About me

I was born in 1998, Miskolc. In my early years, we moved to Felsőzsolca, so I attended kindergarten and elementary school there. Even in kindergarten, I decided that I wanted to do something like this, so I pursued my studies in this direction. I currently live in Felsőzsolca in our family house. In 2020, we opened a sewing workshop, and since then, my passive tasks have included maintaining its offline and online presence. In my free time, I create as much as I can, and I share a lot of my work on social media.
